What a body count can't tell you

The track record

On last year's deaths, AI sits between selfies and sharks. A person choosing what to be afraid of this year should start at the top of Track 1, with cigarettes, drink and the drive home. Counting everything any tracker lists does not move AI a single place: 108 is still fewer deaths than the roads cause in an hour.

The forecast

The people most worried about AI are not claiming it has a body count. They are forecasting one. In a 2023 survey of 2,778 AI researchers, the median respondent gave a 5% chance of extinction-level outcomes, though critics question how representative the sample is. Nuclear weapons scored zero on this benchmark until 6 August 1945.

KillBench measures the first and cannot measure the second. Both can be true at once: the record is tiny and the forecast is not. Past performance is not indicative of future results.

How the counting works

  • Each row is the latest figure its source publishes, so data years run from 2016 to 2025. Where sources disagree the row shows the range, and it is ranked by the range's geometric midpoint.
  • Row names are shorthand. Cars means all road traffic. Mosquitoes counts malaria only. Knives counts homicides only. Gravity is falls.
  • Human baselines depend on what you count. Famine, war, camps and executions are attributed differently by different historians. Each row says what it includes.
  • AI has three counting rules. Confirmed needs an official finding. Alleged adds lawsuits, investigations and press reports. The third rule adds up the longest list anyone keeps for each kind of system: 39 + 65 + 1 + 2 + 1 = 108.
  • Military targeting is not scored. Reporting by +972 Magazine and Local Call, citing six Israeli intelligence officers, said an AI system called Lavender marked up to 37,000 people in Gaza as targets. The Israeli military called the account baseless and says analysts verify each target. Nobody can say how many deaths belong to the software, so the row is blank. If a number is ever attached, it could exceed the rest of AI's row combined.
  • Nothing here is net of lives saved. Cars also carry ambulances, and driver assistance also prevents crashes. This is a count of deaths, not a verdict.
  • The data file is public. killbench.json has every figure, range, note and source on this page.
